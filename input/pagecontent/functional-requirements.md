Functional requirements describe the capabilities the system must have in order to meet the end-users’ needs and achieve tasks within the business process.

These requirements are adapted from Component 8 and Web Annex D of the WHO Digital Adaptation Kit for HIV and customized for Ethiopian Ministry of Health review.

Please note that these are not exhaustive lists and should be modified according to Ministry-approved context and user persona needs.
